## 2. Additions the primary data yields (not in NEW3's doc)

- **India (IN/SEA) economics:** median Y1 RLTV per payer **$14** vs $32 NA / $23 global; D35
  conversion **1.4%** (lowest of all regions); D60 RPI $0.11. Pressure-tests LawMind's ₹-pricing:
  the ₹799+ screen tiers sit in the region with the least per-payer value.
- **Google Play involuntary-cancellation share ~31% vs 14% App Store** — the #1 churn lever for a
  paid tier is billing-failure recovery. Directly relevant to LCC's future billing webhook and
  dunning; no paid tool needed.
- **Hybrid monetization remains rare (10% of apps), concentrated in gaming** — the subscription+
  credit hybrid is defensible but not a default; the data says start with a well-run subscription
  (trial length, Day-0 experience) before layering one-off packs.
