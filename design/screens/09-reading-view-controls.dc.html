Kahkashan Kausar v. State of Bihar
¶ 11 of 28
11
Omnibus allegations against a husband's relatives, unsupported by any specific instance of cruelty, cannot form the basis of a prosecution under section 85 of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita.
12
In Geeta Mehrotra v. State of U.P. this Court observed that mere naming of relatives does not justify trial.
