Verification badge
DECIDED · D
The tick binds to the citation string, not the card, with a gilt ring. It survives being quoted inside a draft, a briefing and a copied citation, which no card-level treatment does. The loud header strip (C) is dropped entirely rather than shown for two sessions — a first-run-only treatment is a thing to maintain forever for a benefit that lasts a week.
Briefing direction
DECIDED · 1g TAKEOVER
Full-screen, ink ground, gilt seal. The memo variant (1h) is beautiful but it reads as one more document in an app full of documents. The takeover is the only screen in Lawmind that changes colour, and that is precisely why arriving at it feels like something happened. 1h's dropped cap and "the one point to win" block are folded into 1g as block 01 — the best idea from the loser survives.
Overruled states
DECIDED · THREE
Set aside (full-bleed caution banner, replaces the header) · partly set aside (banner naming the affected paragraphs, struck through in the text) · doubted (a single line under the citation, no banner). Binary would be a correctness bug in Indian practice, and the citator graph already carries the distinction.
Today with no hearings
DECIDED · NO TASK MODEL
No to-do system. Lawmind derives the one overdue obligation from the matter timeline — a filing due before a listed date, a reply not started — and surfaces it as a single sentence with one action. Advocates already have a diary; we are not competing with it. This keeps the empty state honest without adding an entity.
Checklist persistence
DECIDED · NOT EVENTS
Ticks live on the briefing and sync, but they never become timeline events. A matter timeline is a record of what the court did; polluting it with "printed Antil paras" would make the one genuinely authoritative surface in the app untrustworthy. The matter header shows 3 of 4 prepared and nothing more.
Hindi and citations
DECIDED · ENGLISH, EXPLAINED
Citations stay in English inside Hindi drafts, because that is how they must appear when filed. One line under the language toggle says so, once. No transliteration, no dual rendering — pretending otherwise would produce documents that get returned at the filing counter.
Paywall on hearing day
DECIDED · NEVER BLOCK
Search is never cut off on a day the advocate has a listed hearing. The limit still exists and is still shown, but on a hearing day it becomes advisory. An advocate blocked mid-preparation churns and tells the bar; the marginal cost of a few searches is nothing against that.
Notes vs record
DECIDED · TWO WEIGHTS
Court record renders in Lora on a quote card; private notes render in Inter, indented, in muted ink, prefixed "Your note". Under a firm plan the record is shared and notes are not, by default and without a setting.

What still stands
The Court's observations on misuse of s. 498A and on the need for caution before arrest remain good law and continue to be cited. Only the machinery it created — screening by Family Welfare Committees before arrest — was struck down as beyond the judicial function.
Do not rely · paras 19–20
"In every district one or more Family Welfare Committees be constituted by the District Legal Services Authorities… no arrest should normally be effected till the report of the Committee is received."
