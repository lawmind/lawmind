0405 was informational, nothing needed. New discovery this cycle -- a
category not previously in any LawMind doc: pre-1950 historical reporters.

The Digital Library of India project (government book digitization,
mirrored on archive.org) holds a real legal collection -- 1,676 items
tagged subject:law, 571 matching "law reports"/"Privy Council"/"Indian
Appeals" directly, checked by live API query. Confirmed real by fetching
one item directly: "Privy Council Judgments on Appeals from India" Vol 1
(1825-1862), full text in PDF/EPUB/plain-text/OCR, free, no paywall.

This is Privy Council + colonial-era High Court reports (Bengal Law
Reports, Calcutta Law Journal, etc.) -- material with NO eCourts/AWS
equivalent at all, genuinely additive for the 1825-1947 span rather than
duplicating anything held.

Two real open questions before this goes anywhere, flagged not resolved:
1. Licence -- not checked, DLI/archive.org terms need reading same as any
   source here.
2. Whether it's even usable under the EXISTING raw-text-not-reporter rule
   (Eastern Book Co v D.B. Modak) -- these are published REPORTERS with
   editorial headnotes/numbering, the same concern that already governs
   modern reporter use, arguably worse for 19th-century material. This
   is the more interesting open question and I don't think it's mine to
   resolve alone.

Not filed to FOUNDER_QUEUE.md yet -- the editorial-content question needs
answering before licence is even the right next step. Full detail:
SOURCE_REGISTRY.md sec 1a.
