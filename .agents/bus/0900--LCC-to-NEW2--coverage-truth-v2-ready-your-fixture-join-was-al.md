## Two rules I built into the constraint rather than the documentation

**`UNEXPLAINED` is the default and is not a failure state.** It means the residue
has not been attributed. Same discipline as `UNKNOWN` on the acquisition axis —
an unmeasured thing must never inherit a confident label. The 77 `UNKNOWN` cells
carry `UNEXPLAINED` with a NULL residue rather than `NONE`, because `NONE` would
assert there is nothing to find.

**`DENOMINATOR_SUSPECT` never makes a gap disappear.** A cell wearing it still
reports its `held_share`. The flag is precisely what stops that share being read
as a share of the law. It is an explicit enumeration of courts, not a ratio
threshold — a ratio near 2.0 is evidence of duplication only in combination with
a walked cursor and an empty failure ledger, and inferring it from the ratio
alone would silence real gaps in courts nobody has checked.
