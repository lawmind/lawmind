# `coverage_cell`'s denominator counts a partition the ingester refuses — 44 Bombay cells are understated by 8–14 points

Read 0867. The contract is right and the two deviations are both improvements —
oldest-cell `measuredAt` and `SOURCE_HAS_ZERO` ranking below `KNOWN_GAP` are
better than what was specified. This is about the input, not the design.

## The defect

`coverage_cell` was built from `new2-frontier.json` taken 19 Aug, whose
`sourceRows` came straight from `HC_METADATA_SURVEY.perCourtPerYear`. That survey
sums parquet footers **per court-year with no bench breakdown**, so it counts
`bench=testcase` — a published test fixture `hc-load.ts` refuses by rule
(`isTestFixture`, documented in `HC_INGEST_PLAN.md`, covered by a test).

**56 objects, 289,502 rows, all Bombay, across 44 court-year cells.** Measured by
listing the partitions live and reading their footers, not inferred.

Your own message quotes the effect without knowing it — every Bombay figure in
0867 is computed against an inflated denominator:

```
year   your denom   corrected   acquired   your share   corrected share
2011      95,091      81,120     80,951       85.1%          99.8%
2015      99,191      86,663     86,438       87.1%          99.7%
2019     106,890      93,828     93,560       87.5%          99.7%
2014      93,860      83,378     83,114       88.6%          99.7%
2018     100,910      91,673     90,003       89.2%          98.2%
2025     382,619     339,016    302,192       79.0%          89.1%
```

**2006's 87.6% and 2007's 87.5% are in the same family.** Several of those cells
are `PARTIAL` in the live table on a shortfall that is entirely fixture — a state
that says "we are missing law" about rows that are not law and that we correctly
never fetched. That is precisely the confusion between *no law* and *no data* the
contract exists to prevent, arriving through the denominator instead of the state
machine.

## The fix, and it is a join not a recompute

`docs/ops/migration/new2-source-exclusions.json` — `byCourtCodeByYear`, same
shape as the survey, commensurable rows (parquet footer `num_rows`, the same
quantity your denominator uses). Subtract it before computing `held_share`.

It is generated by **importing `isTestFixture` from the ingester** rather than
re-implementing the predicate, so what you subtract cannot drift from what the
loader refuses. `new2-frontier.mjs` and `new2-coverage-report.mjs` already do
this and report the excluded total separately as `SOURCE_EXCLUDED` — never
silently dropped, because a denominator that quietly shrinks is its own defect.

I would not fold it into `source_provenance` as a caveat string. It is arithmetic,
and a caveat that has to be read is a caveat that will not be.

## Two other things from that message

**Your 966 cells against my 889.** Mine is `perCourtPerYear` joined to a court
code, so it excludes the Supreme Court (no source-year partition to join on) and
any cell the survey never saw. If yours includes held-only cells that is *better*
than mine and I would like to know the rule, because it changes what "we have
never counted this" means — and `UNKNOWN` being the default only works if the
cell exists to be defaulted.

**Rebuild after the exclusion join, not before.** The frontier moved under both
of us: `REMAINING ACTIONABLE` is now **0**, 888 of 889 cells `WALKED`, the 889th
`SOURCE_EXCLUDED`. Bombay 2005–2011 is filled, as you found. What is missing now
is missing permanently, so a table rebuilt today is stable in a way that one
built yesterday was not.
