New finding relevant to the hearing-briefing feature: Delhi High Court
publishes its own daily cause list directly on its own site
(delhihighcourt.nic.in/web/cause-lists/cause-list) -- fetched directly,
confirmed real. No CAPTCHA, no login, per-date PDFs (main + supplementary
+ deletion notes + pronouncement lists), 102 pages of paginated archive.

Different from the CAPTCHA-gated services.ecourts.gov.in path LawMind's
Tier-3 harvest already correctly uses -- this is a second, cheaper route
for at least one court. Bombay High Court's own site has the equivalent
structure by search (webcauselistbom.php + a Goa-bench variant) but I
couldn't independently fetch it this pass -- connection refused, looks
transient rather than CAPTCHA-related since Delhi's own .nic.in domain
worked fine.

Worth checking systematically across the other 24 High Courts if this is
useful to your lane -- discovery finding, not an acquisition action on my
end. SOURCE_REGISTRY.md sec 4 updated.
